StudyBloom — AI Content Creation System 🌷

Project Overview

StudyBloom is a fictional student productivity and lifestyle brand designed for students aged 16–24. This project focuses on building an AI-assisted content strategy to create engaging, relatable and useful Instagram content.

Project Objective

The objective is to develop a structured content creation system using AI-assisted brainstorming, brand voice development, content pillars, hook generation and a 30-day editorial calendar.

Target Audience

- Students aged 16–24
- Students struggling with productivity and time management
- Students looking for study tips, motivation and organisation

Brand Voice

- Friendly
- Motivational
- Relatable
- Supportive
- Creative

Content Pillars

1. Study Tips & Learning — Revision techniques, note-taking, exam preparation and learning strategies.
2. Productivity & Time Management — Planning, routines, focus, time management and procrastination.
3. Student Lifestyle & Motivation — Student experiences, motivation, self-care and relatable student content.

Project Deliverables

- Brand Voice Guidelines
- 3 Content Pillars
- 50 AI-Generated Content Hooks
- Hook Ratings
- 30-Day Instagram Editorial Calendar
- Content Captions
- Structured Notion Content Database

Tools Used

- ChatGPT (AI-assisted ideation and content generation)
- Notion (content planning and organisation)
- GitHub (project documentation and portfolio)

Workflow

1. Define the brand identity and target audience.
2. Develop brand voice guidelines.
3. Identify content pillars.
4. Generate and evaluate content hooks.
5. Create captions and content ideas.
6. Build a 30-day editorial calendar.
7. Organise the project and document it on GitHub.

StudyBloom is a fictional brand created for an internship project and portfolio demonstration.
