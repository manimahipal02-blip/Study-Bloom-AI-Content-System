# StudyBloom Content Pillars

StudyBloom uses three primary content pillars to maintain consistency, variety and relevance for its student audience.

## 1. Study Tips & Learning
**Purpose:** Help students learn more effectively and improve their study techniques.

**Topics:**
- Active recall
- Revision techniques
- Note-taking
- Exam preparation
- Memory techniques

**Content formats:** Reels, carousels and educational Stories.

## 2. Productivity & Time Management
**Purpose:** Help students manage their time, tasks and study routines.

**Topics:**
- Time blocking
- Daily planning
- Productivity routines
- Procrastination
- Organising study tasks

**Content formats:** Reels, carousels, checklists and Stories.

## 3. Student Lifestyle & Motivation
**Purpose:** Connect with students through relatable experiences, motivation and self-care.

**Topics:**
- Student struggles
- Motivation
- Burnout awareness
- Self-care
- College life and relatable moments

**Content formats:** Reels, Stories and relatable carousels.

## Why These Pillars?
These three pillars balance educational value, practical productivity advice and relatable student experiences. Together, they help StudyBloom create diverse and consistent content while connecting with its target audience.
