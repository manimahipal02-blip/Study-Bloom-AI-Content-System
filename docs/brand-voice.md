# Study Bloom Brand Voice Guidelines

## Brand Identity
Study Bloom is a student productivity and lifestyle brand created to help students study smarter, stay organised and feel less overwhelmed.

## Target Audience
- Students aged 16–24
- College and school students
- Students interested in study habits, productivity, motivation and organisation

## Brand Personality
- Friendly
- Supportive
- Relatable
- Motivating
- Creative

## Tone of Voice
Study Bloom communicates in simple, conversational and encouraging language. The brand avoids sounding overly formal or unrealistic. It focuses on practical advice, empathy and small achievable changes.

## Communication Style
- Use short and easy-to-understand sentences.
- Speak directly to students.
- Encourage without pressuring.
- Use relatable student experiences.
- Keep content positive, realistic and helpful.

## Brand Goals
- Encourage consistent study habits.
- Improve student engagement.
- Share practical productivity advice.
- Build a relatable student community.
- Help students manage their time and responsibilities.

## Visual Identity
- Soft Pink: #F8DDE8
- Cream: #FFF8EF
- Lavender: #E6E0F5
- Dark Brown: #4B3B35

## Example Brand Message
"You don't need to have everything figured out today. Start with one small step, and let that be enough."
